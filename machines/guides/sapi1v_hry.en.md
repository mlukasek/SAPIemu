# SAPI-1 V: games and demos

The V set with both graphics cards, CGA-1V and DGD-1V, and YM3812 sound. Everything is on the RAM disk A:.

## How to start

1. After power on press `3` (CP/M from the RAM disk), then type the name of a program and Enter. `DIR` lists all
   files.
2. The games and demos show in the CGA-1V or DGD-1V window, the sound plays at 100 % speed.
3. Joystick: Ctrl+F8 turns on the joystick from the keyboard (arrows, space = fire).

## Games

- `BADAPPLE`: the Bad Apple!! demo (3:39, video from the RAM disk, music on the YM3812).
- `FXS4`: Fuxoft Soundtrack IV, a music demo; keys A to Z choose the tunes.
- `FLAPPY`: Flappy on CGA-1V (dB-SOFT 1984, port from the MZ-800).
- `FLAPDGD`: Flappy on DGD-1V (port by Libor Lasota), played with the joystick: Ctrl+F8, space = fire on the title
  and in the scene selection.
- `BOMBER`: Bomberman (Hudson Soft 1983, port from the MZ-700), space starts the game.
- `BKP`: a Czech text adventure (SAPI version by Libor Lasota); choose the output first, `3` is the AND-1V display.

## Demos and music by Libor Lasota

- `Y1STDEMO`: a demo of the sound card with a picture on DGD-1V; `SNDDEMON`: a sound demo (sound only).
- `CASTLE`, `REMBRAN1`, `REMBRAN2`: pictures on CGA-1V; `CGATEST`, `MPHTEST`: tests of the cards.
- VGM tunes and RAD modules:

```
SPLAY HELLFIRE.VGM
RAT SMJOOP.RAD
```

  `DIR *.VGM` and `DIR *.RAD` list the other tunes.
