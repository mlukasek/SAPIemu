# ZPS-6 (SAPI-1.5)

SAPI-1.5 of 1990: the PKT-1 CPU card, the colour display DBS-1A (64 x 20 characters), a 5.25" floppy with the
MFC-1A controller and CP/M 2.2 62K by Melsoft (Ing. Jan Meloun).

## Try it

1. Click the screen window. The system reports `PKT-1 62K MON-V100690*LCH`, `CP/M V2.2 62K` and the prompt `A>`.
2. `DIR` lists the disk.
3. `SETDBS1` shows the colours of the DBS-1A display and its whole character set.

```
SETDBS1
```
